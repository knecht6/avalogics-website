About NASA SEWP VI

The Solutions for Enterprise-Wide Procurement (SEWP) VI is a Governmentwide Acquisition Contract (GWAC) administered by NASA. It enables all federal agencies and authorized contractors to acquire commercial-off-the-shelf (COTS) information technology products, communications equipment, audio-visual systems, software, cloud solutions, and related professional services.

Advancing NASA's flagship procurement program, SEWP VI expands flexibility by enabling agencies to acquire enterprise-wide professional and managed services without requiring associated hardware or software purchases. Through the SEWP VI Quote Request Tool (QRT) and Fair Opportunity procedures, SEWP VI provides federal agencies a streamlined, fully compliant path to innovative IT solutions.

Avalogics LLC is a NASA SEWP VI Category A prime contract holder, authorized to deliver ITC/AV products, solutions, and related product-based services to any federal agency or authorized contractor using this GWAC vehicle. Our team brings the responsiveness of a small business with the technical depth to support complex federal requirements.

A.1.13 — Fair Opportunity and Requests for Quotes

The following clause appears in Avalogics SEWP VI Contract 80TECH26D0496 per the SEWP VI Statement of Work:

Contractors will be provided a fair opportunity at the individual order level as appropriate per FAR Part 16.505(b), including the SEWP RFQ tools. No documentation for the order selection is required to be submitted with the order. All such documentation is to be maintained by the issuing procurement office.

The Contractor shall not market, quote, or otherwise offer for sale any IT Solutions not listed under this contract, until the said solutions are included in the SEWP database of record and available to all Government end-users.

If the Government issues a Request for Information (RFI) as part of market research, the Contractor may provide items not yet listed on its SEWP contract as part of a market research quote if:

  • All such items are clearly marked as not yet available on the Contractor's SEWP contract; and
  • The Contractor submits a Technology Refreshment request to add those products to its contract.

If the Government issues a Request for Quote (RFQ) or Market Research Request (MRR), the Contractor may only respond with items available on its Contract, and the price of each item shall be no greater than the price in the SEWP VI database of record at the time the quote is issued. If the Contractor has insufficient items on its contract to fully respond to a Formal RFQ, the Contractor must respond with a No Bid.

Unless the RFQ specifically allows for partial quotes, the Contractor must respond fully to all requirements specified in the RFQ.

When submitting a quote to a government end-user, the Contractor must clearly state the length of time the quote is valid. The Contractor shall honor any order submitted within the stated time period of a quote.

When responding to an RFI or RFQ issued from the NASA SEWP RFQ online quoting system, the Contractor must respond as outlined in Attachment C: Contract Holder User Manual (CHUM).

Contract Holders are prohibited from using Government information posted on the NASA SEWP Contract Holder Only Page, such as RFQs, RFIs, etc., for purposes other than proposing on SEWP requirements. This includes Contract Holders providing third parties with SEWP information and requirements for the purpose of assisting companies that are not SEWP Contract Holders with providing unsolicited proposals to meet agency requirements already posted to the NASA SEWP RFQ online quoting system.

Avalogics SEWP VI Ordering Guide

This section provides a step-by-step overview of the SEWP VI ordering process for federal customers placing orders with Avalogics LLC under Contract 80TECH26D0496.

SEWP VI Ordering Process

  1. Identify Your Requirement

    Determine the ITC/AV product or service needed and confirm it falls within SEWP VI Category A scope. Contact Avalogics for assistance with requirements development or market research responses at jose@avalogics.com.

  2. Issue an RFQ Through the SEWP Quote Request Tool (QRT)

    Submit your Request for Quote (RFQ) through the NASA SEWP online quoting system at www.sewp.nasa.gov. All Category A contract holders, including Avalogics, receive the RFQ simultaneously for Fair Opportunity compliance per FAR 16.505(b).

  3. Receive and Evaluate the Avalogics Quote

    Avalogics will respond with a compliant quote that includes itemized pricing, TAA compliance status for each item, delivery terms, warranty details, and a clearly stated quote validity period. All prices are at or below the SEWP VI database of record.

  4. Issue a Delivery Order

    Upon selecting Avalogics, issue a Delivery Order referencing Contract 80TECH26D0496. Include the SEWP Control Number (SCN) provided by NASA SEWP. Avalogics will not fulfill any order until the SCN is received.

  5. Delivery and Acceptance

    Avalogics fulfills the order per the terms of the Delivery Order. A CLIN Verification File is provided with each order for your records. All items are delivered in accordance with applicable TAA, AbilityOne, EPEAT, and ENERGY STAR requirements as specified.

  6. Post-Delivery Support

    For any post-delivery issues, including warranty claims, technical support, damaged or incorrect products, or order status, contact the Avalogics SEWP VI team at jose@avalogics.com or (385) 519-9436. Please have your Delivery Order number and SCN available.
